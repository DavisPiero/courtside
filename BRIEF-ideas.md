# Brief: new things to add

Status (2026-10-18, session 6b): ideas only. Nothing here is built until David picks it. The core-game ideas
(fluid controls, choices that beat each other) are in BRIEF-gameplay.md; this list is everything around the game.
Each has a size: S (an afternoon), M (a session), L (more than a session).

## Reasons to come back tomorrow
1. **Daily challenges (M).** Three small goals a day ("3 dunks in one game", "win on The Cage", "hit 5 perfect
   releases"), each worth 75 to 150 CC, new at midnight. Gives every session a target without the campaign.
2. **Win streak (S).** Wins in a row add 10% each to your coins, up to +50%, shown on the results. Losing resets it.
3. **Rival rematches (M).** Beaten campaign bosses come back a level harder for a bonus and a new line of trash
   talk. Reuses the story scenes.

## Things to collect and show off
4. **Jerseys (M).** Alternate kits for your crew, bought or found in packs, like shoes. Drawn in code, colours only.
5. **Courts to unlock (M).** A new court every so often (a gym with a stage, a snowy park, a school playground)
   bought with CC. Each is one function in js/courts.js.
6. **Trophy room (M).** A shelf screen with every trophy, boss beaten, best minigame score and rare card you own.
   Gives the coins somewhere to point.
7. **Celebrations after a basket (S).** Your equipped celebration plays after a dunk or a buzzer beater, not only
   at the end.

## New ways to play
8. **HORSE (M).** Take turns: make a shot from a spot, the other player has to match it. Fits the 3-point contest's
   code.
9. **21 / King of the Court (M).** Three players, every one for themselves, first to 21.
10. **Two players on one device (L).** One on keyboard, one on a controller, or split touch on an iPad. Still open
    from BRIEF-gameplay.md.
11. **Dunk contest with a crowd vote (S).** The crowd's noise as a sixth judge.

## Making moments stick
12. **Highlight of the game (M).** After each game, the best play (a dunk, an ankle breaker, a buzzer beater)
    replays once from the cutscene camera before the results.
13. **Season stats card (S).** Your hooper's totals (points, dunks, ankle breakers) on the EDIT HOOPER screen.

## My pick
Daily challenges (1) and the win streak (2) first: small, and they give the new coin amounts something to do.
Then the highlight of the game (12), because it makes every game end on a high.
