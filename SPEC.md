# Courtside: spec

## What makes it fun
- **Fast, no-rules basketball.** Possessions are short, the rim is always close, and big
  moments (dunks, ankle breakers, a punch that drops someone) land hard and are over fast.
- **Every press does something you can see.** Dribble moves, slides and shots read
  clearly on the player model, so you feel in control.
- **A choice of feel.** Mayhem for chaos, Clean for straight basketball, and four
  difficulty levels so a beginner and an expert both get a good game.

## Start-up
Loading screen with a spinning pixel basketball, then "How are you playing?": Computer (keyboard
and mouse) or Mobile / Tablet (touch controls). Main menu: a TUTORIAL button under the logo, one big yellow PLAY
button, then Practice, Shop, Inventory, Settings, plus your Court Coins. PLAY slides those buttons out to the left
and brings in Campaign (big, yellow), Quick Play and Minigames (yellow) and BACK from the right, laid out the same
way; coming back from any of them lands on these buttons.
- **Tutorial**: on-court steps on an empty practice court that tick themselves off (move, sprint,
  dribble move, shoot, perfect release, layup/dunk, pass), an alley-oop tip, then real drills: ON FIRE (score
  three in a row), DEFENCE (a slow dummy brings the ball up: steal it or block it), MAYHEM (punch or slide-tackle
  the dummy) and BUZZER BEATERS (a 6-second clock: make a shot that's in the air at 0), then cards about the campaign, your hooper, the NBA career, Quick Play and minigames, coins,
  the Shop and the Inventory. Keyboard or touch wording to match your device.

## Campaigns
Create your hooper (name, skin, hair), then choose your road. **Upgrade your hooper** on EDIT HOOPER: eight stats
(Speed, Accuracy, Range, Finishing, Handling, Defence, Hops, Stamina), +2 an upgrade, prices rising from 50 CC at
50 to about 500 near 99. Your rating is the average. **Height and weight** are free to change on the same screen:
5'6" to 7'0" (default 6'4") and 150 to 300 lb (default 200). Size is a trade-off, not an upgrade, so it doesn't
change your rating: taller means better reach (blocks, contests, steal reach, shooting over people) and dunking but
less speed and handling; heavier means stronger (wins bumps, finishes through contact, up sooner after a knockdown)
but slower, lower jumps and less stamina. A SIZE box under the hooper shows the build's ups and downs as green and
red arrows. The body is drawn taller or broader to match. Measured with the bench: no build is clearly best. New hoopers start at 50; anyone already in the NBA starts at
75 (NBA entry); a hooper part-way through the Street campaign starts 3 under the next game's recommended rating.
Every campaign game shows a recommended rating (Street 50 to 74, NBA 75 to 90).
- **STREET, "King of the Courts"**: outdoor courts (Street, The Cage, Rooftop, Beach), Mayhem games allowed. You're the new kid on the block;
  Dex and Big Lou are your crew. The Block, The City, The Summer Jam. Rivals: Tank (becomes a friend), then
  King and the Kings, who try to poach Dex.
- **NBA, "Road to the Ring"**: locked until you win the Summer Jam (the Street campaign's last boss). Arenas,
  professional rules only (never Mayhem). Coach Ray (coached twenty years, never won a ring, now a scout) finds
  you at the Summer Jam. **You choose your team** from three offers from the league's ten worst teams (ranked by
  the two players you'd join) and play as yourself plus that team's two best players. Dex and Big Lou cheer from
  the stands. After each NBA chapter you get offers from better teams (middle ten, then top ten): stay or move.
  If you'd play your own team, a team close to it in the rankings plays instead. Summer League, The League, The
  Finals. Rival: Zeke Knox, a made-up lottery pick (Washington, then traded to the champs, OKC, for the Finals;
  another team if you play for one of those).
- **Contract**: signing with a new team pays a 1250 CC signing bonus (staying pays none). The contract pays once
  a day, the first time the menu opens that day ("PAYDAY!"): 250 a day, 440 after NBA chapter 1, 625 after
  chapter 2, 940 as champion. Missed days add up to a week.
- **NBA games** are four 30-second quarters (about two minutes); goals are scaled to fit.
- **Getting harder**: Street runs Rookie to All-Star with Hall of Famer only for its last two games; NBA runs
  Pro/All-Star to Hall of Famer. Dex and Big Lou improve with every boss beaten, up to six times; you improve by
  upgrading.
Each has 3 chapters x 6 games with 3 stars (win, twist goal, style goal). Winning unlocks the next game.
**The story is told in scenes**: two characters on screen, the speaker steps forward in a pose that fits the
line, text types out, tap to go on, SKIP to jump. Each chapter opens with a title card and scene (once; replay
with CHAPTER STORY), every game has a scene before it and after it (win or loss), and beating a boss plays the
chapter's ending. During campaign games your crew, Coach Ray, the announcer and the rival talk in a speech
bubble under the scoreboard (dunks, ankle breakers, comebacks, half time, close finishes).
Beating a chapter's boss for the first time wins a free All-Star Pack (open it from the results, or later from
the Shop's Packs tab). Campaign progress was reset once for the new story (2026-10-17); coins and animations kept.

## Career League
Opens once you've won the NBA Finals: a third card on the Campaign screen. Season after season with your hooper
and your NBA team's best two (the same team your contract pays from).
- **Pre-season:** stay with your team or take one of three offers (the best ten teams after a good season, the
  middle ten after missing the playoffs); moving pays the 1250 CC signing bonus.
- **Season:** 12 games, four 30-second quarters, Arena, professional rules. One is a rivalry game against Zeke
  Knox, who signs with one of the best ten. Standings for all 30 teams; every other game is simulated from team
  ratings. All-Star difficulty; Hall of Famer against the best ten from season 2, against last season's champion
  and in the Finals, and against everyone while you are the defending champion. The best ten teams get 1.5
  rating points better each season (up to 8). Recommended rating 90, +2 a season up to 99.
- **Playoffs:** the top 8 (1v8, 4v5, 2v7, 3v6), best of 3, the Finals best of 5. Other series are played out at
  once. Missing out or losing ends the season and the rest is simulated to crown a champion.
- **Rewards:** +50 a league win (on top of the usual coins), 500 for making the playoffs, 750 a series won, a ring
  pays 2000 more plus a free Hall of Fame Pack. Rings are counted; past seasons are listed in the pre-season.
- **The hub:** next game (opponent, their best two, difficulty) and PLAY, then STANDINGS (a playoff line under 8th),
  SCHEDULE (results) and PLAYOFFS (the bracket). Part 2 (not built): All-Star Weekend, awards, story scenes,
  trophy room.

## Accounts
An ACCOUNT button on the main menu (your picture and, when signed in, your username) opens the **account page**:
a profile card (your picture, your name, your title, a gear), your numbers (rating, rings, coins, moves and shoes
collected, wins) and a **Showcase** of four open slots: each holds any move or pair of shoes you own (a chooser
with MOVES and SHOES tabs; picking one that's already shown swaps them). Moves play on a loop. Guests see the same page
as GUEST with a SIGN IN link. Tap the picture, the title or a showcase slot to choose.
- **Pictures:** 14 pixel icons drawn in code (ball, flame, crown, star, trophy, lightning, hoop, diamond, rocket,
  your kicks, your hooper's face); Ring (a Career League ring), Pumpkin and Ghost (Halloween quests) are earned.
- **Titles:** Rookie, then earned: Hooper (10 games), Street King, Champion, Ring Chaser, Pickpocket (50 steals),
  High Flyer (100 dunks), Sharpshooter (100 threes), Ankle Breaker (100), Collector (50 moves), Sneakerhead (20 shoes),
  High Roller (10,000 CC), Pumpkin King (every Halloween quest, Limited). Lifetime totals count from session 8.
  **Developer** (rainbow, the card turns purple) belongs to the account named King only.
- **The gear** opens ACCOUNT SETTINGS: change password, sign out, delete account. Signed out it opens sign in /
  create account. Delete asks "DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT?" with NO, KEEP IT as the big button and YES unlocking after 3 s.
- Picture, title and showcase are in the save, so they go to your account; nobody else can see them yet.
Supabase: email, username and password, no parent emails. Create an account (email, a unique username of 3 to 16
letters, numbers or _, a password of 8+ with a letter and a number; then confirm by email), sign in with email and
password, forgot password (an emailed link).
- The game still saves on the device first, so it plays offline; when signed in, the save goes to your account a
  few seconds after each change. Another device's newer save wins when you open the game.
- Signing in with progress on the device adds it to the account: coins added together, animations, shoes and
  codes combined, the best stars, scores and hooper stats kept; a code used in both saves pays once.
- Signing out clears the device back to a fresh save (keeping today's daily so it can't be claimed twice).
- Each player can only read and write their own save (row-level security, `supabase/setup.sql`).

## Daily
A ★ DAILY button next to your coins on the main menu (a red dot while today's reward is waiting). It opens by
itself the first time the menu shows each day.
- **Daily reward:** a 7-day ladder, one step each day you play (missing a day doesn't reset it), then it starts
  again: 100 CC, 150 CC, Street Pack, 250 CC, Shoebox, 400 CC, All-Star Pack. Packs and boxes wait in the Shop.
- **Daily challenges:** three a day (easy 75 CC, medium 100, hard 150), the same for everyone on the same date,
  new at midnight. Quick Play, campaign games and minigames count (not Practice). A challenge pays as soon as it's
  done, shown on the results screen; punches don't cut it. Examples: play 2 games, dunk 6 times, win by 10 or more.

## Court Coins (CC), the Shop and the Inventory
Earn CC for winning and for style (perfect releases, dunks, ankle breakers, threes, steals, blocks), plus new
stars and first wins in the campaign, plus 125 CC for every finished Quick Play game, minigames and your NBA
contract. (A win pays 38, a loss 13; every reward went up about a quarter in session 6b, prices did not.) Spend it in the Shop and on hooper upgrades. The **Shop** has five tabs:
- **Daily Shop**: five single animations (a featured Iconic or Legendary, sometimes Mythic, rarely Godlike; an
  Epic, two Rares, a Common) and one shoe, the same for everyone in the same hour, **new every hour** with a
  countdown. About one hour in 40 the featured slot is Celestial. Prices: Common 120, Rare 300, Epic 700, Legendary
  1400, Iconic 2000, Mythic 4000, Godlike 10000, Celestial 20000.
  Shoes: 60 to 700, Mythic 2000, Godlike 5000.
- **Packs**: random animations with the odds shown: Street Pack (150 CC, 1 card), Pro Pack (400, 2, one Rare+),
  All-Star Pack (900, 3, one Epic+, 1% Mythic), Hall of Fame Pack (2000, 3, one Legendary, 3.5% Mythic, 0.5%
  Godlike, 0.2% Celestial). New animations 3x as likely. Free packs you've won show OPEN FREE.
- **Player Packs** (1200 CC): Jordan, Kobe, Curry, Iverson, Shaq, LeBron. One of that player's three ICONIC
  signature moves plus a bonus card (Rare 60%, Epic 32%, Legendary 8%).
- **Shoeboxes**: one mystery shoe each: Shoebox (100 CC), Premium Shoebox (300), Grail Box (800, the only box
  with Mythic 5% and Godlike 1%). Unboxing: the box shakes, the lid flies off, tissue paper bursts out and the
  shoe card rises out of the box and builds up like a pack card. The three boxes are pixel models: a brown
  cardboard Shoebox with a size label, a matte black Premium box with a gold band and star, and a purple Grail
  Box with glowing trim, a gold clasp and twinkles. The lid blasts off and light pours out of the box.
- **Codes**: type a code for a reward, once per save, case sensitive. On touch devices the box uses Courtside's
  own on-screen keyboard (capitals and numbers), never the iPad's. LAUNCHED: the Limited animation
  (Liftoff) and 1000 CC. SNEAKERHEAD: the Limited sneaker (Launched) and a free Premium Shoebox. BIG LEAGUE: 2000 CC (the on-screen
  keyboard has a SPACE key). HACKER: 10,000 CC and the Limited Hacker sneaker. Codes are stored scrambled, but the game is public, so a determined player could find them.
**Opening a pack** is a show: the pack shakes, its top strip rips off and light pours out (white for Common and
Rare, the best card's rarity colour from Epic up, so you know something good is coming), the cards slide up out of
the pack face down, and you tap each one. A card
shakes and its glow climbs through the rarity colours (with a rising tick) before it flips; sometimes a low card
flickers one step higher first. Epic and up flash; Legendary and up shake the screen, burst confetti and
show a banner. REVEAL ALL plays them quickly. Duplicates turn into coins.
107 animations in five categories (dunks, layups, dribble moves, jump shots, celebrations). Rarities, low to high:
Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Iconic (18 player signatures), Mythic (6: Honey Dip, Double 360, Yo-Yo, Skywalker,
Angel Glide layup, Fire Breather celebration), Godlike (4: Moonwalk dunk from way out, Levitate, Heaven's Gate shot,
Phantom double spin), **Celestial** (2, rarer than Godlike, cycling cyan-violet-pink-mint: Comet dunk from way out,
Supernova celebration), **Limited** (purple; once called Gametime; from codes and events only, never sold): the
Liftoff dunk, the Witching Hour dunk (Halloween 2026).
**Rare moves glow** (js/fx.js), on your team only: Legendary twinkles, Mythic leaves a glowing trail, Godlike
wears a golden aura and a ring of light at the feet, Celestial spirals stars and colour and bursts at the finish;
a rare jump shot leaves a glowing trail on the ball. Witching Hour rises on a purple-green spiral of ghost-fire and
bats burst off the rim; Fire Breather breathes flame; Supernova bursts into stars. (Not shown in the Animation Lab.)
**Shoes**: 45 real shoes (no logos) in Common to Godlike (Air Mag, Jordan 4 'Undefeated'), plus Courtside's own
Limited pairs: Launched (purple and black, rocket flame, glowing sole) and Jack-O (Halloween 2026: pumpkin orange
and black, a jack-o'-lantern face, a ghost-green glowing sole) and Hacker (code HACKER: jet black, green code falling
down the side, a glitch in the heel, a terminal-green glowing sole); your whole team wears
the pair you pick. The **Inventory** equips what you own (one per category, up to 5 extra dribble moves, a pair
of shoes) and **sells** animations for a quarter of their shop price (press twice; not starters, Limited or
anything equipped). No real money, ever.

## Limited events
**Halloween 2026** (until midnight as 1 November begins, on the device's clock; `?date=yyyy-mm-dd` tests a date):
a pumpkin HALLOWEEN banner under the coins on the main menu (quest number and time left) opens a quest track, like a
tiny battle pass. 8 quests, one at a time, in order, at most one per game, counted only in Quick Play games on
**Haunted Hollow** (a limited court: a graveyard pumpkin patch under a full moon, dead trees, gravestones, an iron
fence, flickering jack-o'-lanterns, bats, low green fog, orange lines): play a game (150 CC), score 20 (150 + the
Pumpkin picture), 3 steals (200), dunk 5 times (250 + **Jack-O shoes**), 3 threes (250), win (300 + the Ghost
picture), win by 10 (300), win on All-Star or harder (400 + the **Witching Hour** dunk + the Pumpkin King title).
2,000 CC in all. Rewards are kept for good; after the end the banner, the court and the quests disappear.

## Minigames
3-POINT CONTEST (60 s, five racks of five around the arc, last ball 2 points, you just shoot; 5 CC a point),
DUNK CONTEST (three dunks from half court, five judges out of 10 each for rarity, speed, dribble moves on the way
in and long take-offs; each round uses the next dunk you own, repeats lose points; CC = 0.625 x the score),
1-ON-1 (you against a top-ten team's star, full court on The Cage, first to 11; 100 CC a win, 25 a loss). New best +30.

## Showtime
- **On Fire**: three baskets in a row without the other team scoring: flaming ball, green zone 1.5x as big (the
  AI shoots better) until the other team scores. You can see it: a pulsing red outline round every player on that
  team and round the ball, the shot meter's green zone glows orange with ON FIRE over it, and an orange flash
  when it starts.
- **Alley-oops**: bring the ball up to the 3-point line and your most open teammate cuts hard to the rim; their
  defender is caught watching the ball for a moment (shorter on harder levels). Teammates also cut now and then on
  their own. When one is open near the rim a gold arrow shows over them and PASS throws the lob (on touch the PASS
  button turns gold and says OOP!). They catch it and dunk. About 5 chances a game on Pro (it was 1).
- **Buzzer beaters**: a shot in the air at 0:00 still counts; the game slows down while it flies. In at the end of
  the game: BUZZER BEATER!
- **The win**: winning Quick Play or an NBA campaign game plays a celebration (about 4 s): your team at centre
  court, your star (your hooper in the campaign) lifts a trophy, confetti, the losers walk off. Winning the NBA
  Finals: CHAMPIONS with a taller gold trophy. Tap, Space, Enter or click skips.

## Animation
Every move is a keyframed clip with eased timing, blended and smoothed so nothing snaps; moves are
exaggerated so they read in pixel art. Settings > Animation Lab shows any move at 1×, ½× or ¼× speed
from the front, side or back.

## Touch controls
Floating joystick on the left (edge = sprint), right-thumb cluster: one big button plus two, with
labels that change with possession (SHOOT/PASS/MOVE, or JUMP-BLOCK/SWITCH/STEAL). Punch and Slide
in Mayhem only. Pause and camera top-left. Bigger menus on touch; compact layout on phones; every panel stays full size and
scrolls if it's taller than the screen, with its buttons pinned at the bottom, except the Shop, Inventory and
Settings, whose BACK sits at the end of the page (a tabbed screen keeps one size);
"turn sideways" prompt in portrait. No accidental zoom: double taps and pinches are blocked, and the
page snaps back if a browser zooms anyway.

## Modes and options (chosen on the setup screen)
Mode, court and difficulty are small chips at the bottom showing the current choice; tapping one opens a pop-up
with every option and what it means.
- **Mode:** (starts on Clean; hover or tap for a description) Mayhem (punch, slide, blood) or Clean (no punch, slide or blood: just dribble,
  pass, shoot, steal, block). Ankle-breaker stuns from dribble moves stay in both.
- **Court:** Arena (official indoor court, crowd, jumbotron), Street (asphalt at dusk, chain-link
  fences, pole hoops, chain nets, no crowd), Rooftop (a painted court on a tower at night, the city below,
  a parapet with a railing, a water tower), Beach (boardwalk court at sunset, sand, palms, string lights, the
  ocean and a lifeguard tower) or The Cage (under a highway bridge, graffiti wall, tall fence, orange lights).
  Haunted Hollow (LIMITED) shows only during Halloween 2026. All outdoor courts use pole hoops. Drawn in js/courts.js.
- **Difficulty:** Rookie (easy), Pro (normal), All-Star (hard), Hall of Famer (expert).
  Changes opponent speed, shooting, steal attempts, defensive tightness and basketball sense (getting the ball out
  of pressure, attacking when you're beaten, shooting when open, keeping the ball away from you, sprinting back on
  defence). **Your STEAL** is a quick arm swipe (no crouch: you keep running at full speed): one swipe every 0.6 s.
  Since session 8 it works about a third more often on every level, and a swipe **from behind** (chasing the
  handler down) works a bit over half as well as one on the ball side (it was a quarter). It still mostly fails
  when the ball is on the far side of the handler or mid dribble move, and better teams are harder to strip. On
  harder levels a miss can leave you reaching ("REACHED!"): your next swipe waits 1.2 s, but you never slow down.
  Better teams' defenders get their ankles broken less.
  **Adaptive AI:** the further ahead you get (from 4 points, full at 20) and the later in the game, the sharper the
  other team plays ("THEY'RE ADJUSTING!"); it eases off if they catch up. Not in Practice or the contests.
  Measured by `./tools/bench.sh`: a bot that plays like a good kid (sprints, dribble moves, human-timed shots,
  mashes STEAL) wins 3-minute games by about +44 on Rookie, +19 on Pro, +3 on All-Star, and loses by about
  11 on Hall of Famer (session 9, 30 games a level; the bot throws an oop when the gold arrow shows) (it won by 88, 69, 62 and 43 before session 6c). Steals were made easier in 6d and session 8
  (session 8 added about 3 to 6 points a game).
- **Bumping:** bodies can't overlap, but the player you control slides round anyone they brush past and keeps most
  of their speed. Defenders never try to stand closer than bumping range.
- **Your hooper's stats all count in games:** Speed (run speed), Accuracy (green zone size and jump shots), Range,
  Finishing (open dunks, contested layups), Handling (ankle breakers, harder to steal from), Defence (steals,
  contests), Hops (jump height for blocks, dunking, blocks), Stamina.
- **League:** a switch at the top: NBA (today's teams) or HALL OF FAME (each franchise's three best players
  of all time, rated at their peak, in js/hof.js). Click a team's name for a grid of all 30.
- **Quick Play length:** four 30-second quarters.
- **Teams:** all 30 NBA teams (2026-27), each team's three most popular players, split-screen select
  (see `Reference/`). Each player's height, speed, shooting, finishing, handling, defence and blocking
  shape how they play.
- **Shooting:** the shot meter's green zone shrinks as defenders close in, more on harder difficulties.
  **Leaning shots:** hold a direction as you go up and you leap that way: back (away from the rim) is a fadeaway,
  sideways a side leap, toward the rim a leaner. A leaning shot is harder to contest but its green zone is a little
  smaller, so it pays off when someone's on you (fadeaway: 85% size, a defender counts 60%; side leap 88%/72%;
  leaner 95%/85%). Your controlled player only. In the Animation Lab under Movement and actions.
  The AI doesn't block your jump shots; tall players are hard to block.
- **Range:** a perfect release always goes in, from anywhere (David, session 4). Past about 26 ft the green
  zone shrinks (under half by half court, a sliver past it), so long ones are hard to hit; your hooper's Range
  stat shrinks it less. Releases that aren't perfect fall off with distance (js/core.js DIFF half/deep/full).
- **Close to the rim:** inside about 6 ft you always get a layup (or a dunk when open and you can dunk), never
  a jump shot. With a defender on you a close layup can roll off the rim, more often on harder levels.
- **Mayhem balance:** every punch you throw cuts that game's coins by 6% (up to half), shown on the results;
  punches a "drop N players" goal needs are free. The slide carries about twice as far as before. Anyone
  knocked down by a punch or slide is up after about 1.4 seconds.
- **Hall of Fame league** teams wear gold trim, and the team select screen turns gold.
- **Settings:** video (pixel size, lighting, fog, bloom (off by default, soft), reflections, colour grade, vignette, scanlines,
  grain, shake), audio, gameplay (camera, name tag, shot meter), controls. Saved in the browser.

## Presentation rules
- Crisp, high-res pixel art: the world renders at low resolution and scales by whole pixels. Pixel UI
  type (Jersey 10) that stays readable. Realistic body proportions, scaled by each player's real height.
- Only the player you control has a name, in plain white pixel letters above their head. No names
  or numbers on any jersey.
- Steals are instant: no steal cutscene. Dunk cutscenes are short (about 1.7 seconds) and have no text.
- The ball is drawn inside the holding player's sprite, so it goes behind the body or sits in the hand correctly;
  a loose ball bounces off backboards and rims.
- Sounds: menu clicks and hover ticks, garble talking in story scenes (a pitch per character), coins, pops,
  sneaker squeaks on hard cuts, backboard thuds, fire, crowd cheer, horn.
- Dribble moves (E) cycle through crossover, between the legs, behind the back and spin,
  each animated on the model.
- No music for now (removed 2026-10-17). Sound effects only.

## Not building
Fouls, free throws, out of bounds, substitutions, franchise management (trades, drafts, ageing), online play
(for now; see BRIEF-online.md), chat between players (ever), real-money purchases (ever), more than 3v3.
